# Guideline: Breathing Difficulty Intake Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Severe breathing difficulty combined with low oxygen saturation (below 90%) is a medical emergency.
- Breathing difficulty in a patient with a known asthma history should be routed urgently to Pulmonology, even without other red-flags, due to elevated risk of rapid deterioration.
- Breathing difficulty combined with chest pain should be treated per the chest-pain emergency guideline.
- Always ask about severity, asthma history, associated chest pain, and oxygen saturation if a pulse oximeter reading is available.
