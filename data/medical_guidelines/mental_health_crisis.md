# Guideline: Evaluation of Mental Health & Psychiatric Concerns in Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Immediate risk of self-harm, suicidal ideation with intent, aggressive behavior, or acute psychosis is a psychiatric emergency requiring immediate safe supervision and emergency psychiatry referral.
- Severe acute panic attacks, overwhelming anxiety, hyperventilation, or extreme acute distress should be evaluated urgently in a calm, secure setting to rule out medical mimics (e.g. arrhythmias or pulmonary embolism) and provide psychiatric support.
- Mild, chronic anxiety, mild stress, or insomnia without acute distress or self-harm thoughts should be routed as standard priority to outpatient behavioral health.
- Always verify the patient is currently in a safe environment.
