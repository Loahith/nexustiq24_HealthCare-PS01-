# Guideline: Evaluation of Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) Conditions

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Inability to swallow secretions (drooling), airway stridor, severe throat swelling, or difficulty breathing indicates potential epiglottitis or peritonsillar abscess requiring immediate emergency escalation.
- Uncontrolled, severe epistaxis (nasal bleeding) not responding to direct pressure requires urgent/emergency management.
- Severe otalgia (ear pain), bloody/purulent ear discharge with fever, or mastoid tenderness warrants urgent evaluation by Otolaryngology.
- Mild sore throat without respiratory distress, mild ear discomfort, nasal congestion, or mild rhinitis are classified as standard priority.
