# Guideline: Evaluation of Dermatological Conditions, Rashes & Burns

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Second or third-degree burns covering significant body surface, circumferential burns, facial burns, or burns involving blistering and skin peeling require urgent or emergency assessment.
- Rapidly spreading, painful erythema, purpura, petechiae, or skin sloughing accompanied by high fever or systemic signs warrant immediate emergency or urgent escalation.
- Localized allergic rashes, contact dermatitis, mild burns, minor eczema, or steady pruritus without systemic illness should be classified as standard priority and routed to Dermatology.
- Assess onset speed, blistering, open skin breakdown, pain severity, and accompanying fever.
