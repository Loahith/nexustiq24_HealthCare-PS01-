# Guideline: Fever Intake Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- A temperature above 103°F (39.4°C) is considered high-grade fever and warrants urgent evaluation.
- Fever persisting beyond 5 days without improvement warrants urgent evaluation even if the temperature itself is moderate.
- Fever combined with breathing difficulty or chest pain should be escalated regardless of temperature reading.
- Mild fever (under 100.4-103°F) lasting less than 3 days with no red-flag symptoms can generally be routed to standard-priority general medicine review.
- Always record exact temperature and duration in days; do not rely on the patient's subjective description alone.
