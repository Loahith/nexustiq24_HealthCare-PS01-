# Guideline: Evaluation of Ophthalmic Complaints & Eye Injuries

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Chemical splashes into the eye (acids, alkalis, industrial cleaners) require immediate emergency copius eye irrigation and immediate emergency escalation.
- Penetrating ocular trauma, foreign bodies embedded in the globe, or acute sudden severe vision loss are surgical emergencies requiring immediate emergency referral.
- Acute eye pain with severe headache, haloes around lights, and nausea may indicate acute angle-closure glaucoma requiring immediate evaluation.
- Mild superficial conjunctivitis ("pink eye"), mild foreign body sensation without trauma, discharge, or mild eyelid styes are categorized as standard priority.
