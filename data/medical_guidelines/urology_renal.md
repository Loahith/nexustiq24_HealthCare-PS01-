# Guideline: Evaluation of Urological & Renal Complaints

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Acute urinary retention (complete inability to void with agonizing suprapubic pain) is an urgent condition requiring prompt catheterization.
- Severe unilateral flank pain radiating to the groin (renal colic / suspected nephrolithiasis) or gross hematuria with blood clots warrants urgent evaluation in Urology.
- Dysuria accompanied by high fever (>102°F), rigors, costovertebral angle tenderness, or vomiting indicates potential acute pyelonephritis requiring urgent care.
- Mild dysuria, slight urinary frequency, or mild lower urinary tract symptoms without fever, flank pain, or gross hematuria can be triaged as standard priority.
