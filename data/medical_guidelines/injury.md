# Guideline: Musculoskeletal Injury Intake Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Inability to bear weight or walk after a lower-limb injury is a strong indicator for urgent orthopedic evaluation.
- Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding after any injury is an emergency regardless of other factors.
- Visible deformity or significant swelling suggests possible fracture and should be routed urgently to Orthopedics.
- Minor injuries where the patient can walk normally, with no bleeding, deformity, or significant swelling, can generally be handled as standard-priority orthopedic review.
- Always ask about location, bleeding, ability to walk, and swelling/deformity.
