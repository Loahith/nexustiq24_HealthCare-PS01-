# Guideline: Evaluation of Chest Pain in Intake Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Chest pain accompanied by shortness of breath, dizziness, sweating, or pain radiating to the arm/jaw is considered a red-flag combination and should be treated as a potential emergency requiring immediate escalation.
- Chest pain alone, without red-flag accompanying symptoms, still warrants prompt clinical evaluation but may not require emergency escalation.
- Intake staff should never attempt to distinguish cardiac from non-cardiac chest pain; that determination belongs to a clinician.
- Always ask about duration, severity (mild/moderate/severe), and any associated breathing difficulty or dizziness.
