# Guideline: Abdominal Pain Intake Triage

Source: Adapted general intake triage reference (non-diagnostic, for staff guidance only).

- Severe abdominal pain, described by the patient as severe or unbearable, warrants urgent Gastroenterology evaluation.
- Abdominal pain accompanied by persistent vomiting should be treated as urgent due to risk of dehydration or obstruction.
- Mild abdominal pain without red-flag accompanying symptoms can generally be routed to standard-priority review.
- Always ask about exact location, presence of vomiting, associated fever, and duration in days.
